Cards & Ranks

A simple visual system for discipline and promotion.

YELLOW CARD

Three strikes

Three yellow cards result in suspension from the group.

BLUE CARD

Direct suspension

A blue card results in direct suspension from the group.

BLACK CARD

Permanent ban

A black card results in permanent removal from the group.

GREEN CARD

Promotion

A green card is awarded when a member is promoted to a higher position.
