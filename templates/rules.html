The Constitution

Rules

Keep the group organized, respectful and functional.

  1. If anyone wants to shift their role, they need approval from the manager and leader.
  2. Voting for changes in the group should be based on maturity and thoughtful discussion.
  3. Every member should sit close to the others in class when practical.
  4. PR plans are made by the leader and manager.
  5. Members leaving the group should submit a resignation letter to the manager for approval.

More rules can be added here as the group evolves.
